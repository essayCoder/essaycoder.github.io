2. 常见问题（FAQ）

2.1 澄清镜分析提示「需要先同意」？

澄清镜、练习场会调用第三方 AI（DeepSeek）。首次使用任一 AI 功能时，App 会弹窗说明「发送什么 / 发给谁 / 用途」，需点击「同意并继续」后方可使用。不同意时，本地功能（日记、证据、图书馆等）仍可正常使用，仅 AI 功能不可用。可在「设置 → 隐私与同意」中重新查看或撤回同意。

2.2 粘贴对话后分析失败 / 一直转圈？

  • 检查网络：AI 分析需要联网，请在 Wi-Fi 或蜂窝网络下重试。
  • 文本过长：单次建议不超过约 4000 字，可分次提交。
  • 截图不清晰：请确保截图文字清晰、未遮挡，且体积不超过 10MB。
  • 仍失败：完全退出 App 后重新打开，或更新到最新版本。

2.3 忘记隐私锁密码怎么办？

出于安全设计，隐私锁密码不存储在服务器、无法找回。连续输错或选择「忘记密码」会清空本机全部本地数据（日记、证据、练习记录）。开启「云同步备份（VIP）」的数据可重新下载恢复；未开启云同步的数据将无法找回，请谨慎操作。

2.4 数据存在哪里？会不会泄露？

日记、证据、练习记录默认仅存于你的设备（加密）。仅当你主动使用 AI 功能时，输入内容才会发给 DeepSeek；分析完成后原文即焚、不留存。详情见隐私政策。

2.5 如何导出证据 / PDF？

进入「证据拱廊」，选择需要导出的条目，点击「导出 PDF」即可保存到本机文件或分享。PDF 仅包含你选择的内容，不会上传。

2.6 云同步备份（VIP）说明

开启后，本地数据会加密传输至我们的同步服务，用于多设备恢复。你可随时在「设置 → 云同步」中关闭或删除云端备份。关闭后云端数据按约定删除。

2. Frequently Asked Questions

2.1 Clarity Mirror says "consent required"?

Clarity Mirror and the Practice Field call a third-party AI (DeepSeek). The first time you use any AI feature, the app shows a notice explaining what is sent, to whom, and why, and you must tap "Agree & Continue". Without consent, local features (diary, evidence, library) still work — only AI features are disabled. You can review or withdraw consent under Settings → Privacy & Consent.

2.2 Analysis fails / keeps spinning after I paste a conversation?

  • Check your connection: AI analysis needs internet. Retry on Wi-Fi or cellular.
  • Text too long: keep each submission under ~4,000 characters; split longer ones.
  • Screenshot unclear: ensure text is legible and unobstructed, under 10MB.
  • Still failing: force-close the app and reopen, or update to the latest version.

2.3 Forgot my privacy-lock passcode?

By design, the privacy-lock passcode is not stored on any server and cannot be recovered. After repeated wrong attempts or choosing "Forgot passcode", all local data on this device is erased (diary, evidence, practice logs). Data backed up via Cloud Sync (VIP) can be re-downloaded; data without cloud sync cannot be recovered. Please proceed with care.

2.4 Where is my data stored? Can it leak?

Diary, evidence, and practice logs stay encrypted on your device by default. Your input is sent to DeepSeek only when you actively use an AI feature; originals are discarded after analysis and never retained. See the Privacy Policy for details.

2.5 How do I export evidence / PDF?

Open Evidence Gallery, select the items to export, and tap "Export PDF" to save or share. The PDF contains only what you select and is never uploaded.

2.6 Cloud Sync backup (VIP)

When enabled, local data is transmitted encrypted to our sync service for multi-device recovery. You can turn sync off or delete the cloud backup anytime under Settings → Cloud Sync. Cloud data is deleted as agreed after you disable sync.
