隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 16 日 · 适用于应用「NPD避雷针（Narcissist Check）」iOS 与 Android 版本。政策如有更新，以本页面为准。

一句话概括

你的日记、证据与练习记录只存在你自己的手机上。仅当你主动使用「澄清镜」或「练习场」这类 AI 功能，并且你明确同意后，你输入的内容才会发送给第三方 AI 服务商 DeepSeek 用于生成分析；原文分析后立即删除，不留存、不用于广告或用户画像。你可以在设置中随时撤回同意。

1. 概述

本政策说明 NPD避雷针（Narcissist Check）如何收集、使用、存储与保护你的信息。我们的核心原则是「本地优先」：你的记录默认只存在你自己的设备上，不上传至我们的服务器。本应用不要求注册账号。

2. 我们收集的信息

  • 你主动输入的内容：锚点日记（文字、图片、语音）、澄清镜中粘贴的对话文本与上传的聊天截图、离开的理由、练习记录、应用设置偏好。这些内容默认只保存在你的设备上。
  • 订阅状态：来自 App Store / Google Play 的会员状态，用于解锁功能。我们不会经手你的支付卡信息。
  • 我们不会要求你提供姓名、手机号、邮箱或任何可直接识别你身份的信息。

3. AI 功能与第三方数据共享（重点）

发送什么

仅当你使用 AI 功能时：你粘贴或输入的对话文本、你上传的聊天截图（澄清镜），以及你在练习场中选择的场景与每一轮输入的回应。

发送给谁

DeepSeek（deepseek.com），一家第三方 AI 服务商。除 DeepSeek 与应用商店支付平台外，我们不会把你的内容提供给任何其他第三方。

如何使用

仅用于生成当次的分析结果或练习反馈。原文在分析完成后立即删除，我们与 DeepSeek 均不会将其用于广告、用户画像或模型训练。

同意机制

  • 首次打开应用时，应用会以独立页面说明上述「发送什么、发给谁、如何使用」，你必须显式选择「同意共享」或「暂不（仅本地使用）」才能进入应用。
  • 选择「暂不」不影响你使用全部本地功能（日记、证据、图书馆等）；仅当你之后主动使用 AI 功能时，应用会再次弹出说明并请求授权。
  • 你可以随时在「设置 - AI 数据共享」中查看授权状态、开启或撤回同意。

第三方保护

DeepSeek 作为数据接收方，对其处理的用户数据提供不低于本政策的保护标准（详见 DeepSeek 官方隐私政策）。除生成本次分析结果所必需外，我们与 DeepSeek 均不会将你的对话内容用于广告、用户画像或模型训练以外的其他目的。请勿在分析中提交与用途无关的敏感个人信息。

4. 数据存储与安全

  • 本地数据以加密方式存储在你的设备上（iOS 钥匙串 / Android 加密存储，并叠加应用层加密）。
  • 隐私锁（PIN 与面容 / 触控 ID）保护应用入口；忘记 PIN 会清空本机全部数据（这是设计使然，我们无法帮你找回）。
  • 本应用当前版本不提供云同步服务，你的数据不会上传至开发者的任何服务器；未来若提供云同步，我们会在开启前单独说明并另行征得你的同意。

5. 系统权限

  • 照片库：用于选择聊天截图进行沟通模式分析。图片仅本次分析使用，分析后立即删除。
  • 麦克风：用于录制锚点日记的语音记录。录音仅保存在你的手机上。
  • 面容 ID / 触控 ID：用于解锁隐私锁。生物特征数据由系统处理，应用无法访问。

你可以随时在系统设置中撤销任何权限，撤销后对应功能不可用，不影响其他功能与本地数据。

6. 订阅与支付

订阅通过苹果 App Store 或 Google Play 内购完成，由对应平台处理支付，我们仅获取订阅状态用于解锁会员功能。续费、退款与恢复购买遵循各应用商店政策。

7. 数据保留与删除

  • 本地数据保留在你的设备上，直至你主动删除或卸载应用。卸载应用即永久删除全部本地数据、无法恢复，如需保留请先在应用内导出。
  • 「设置 - 清空数据」可一键删除全部日记、证据与设置，操作不可恢复。
  • 发送给 DeepSeek 的内容不构成保留：分析完成后即被删除，我们不留存副本。

8. 未成年人

本应用内容涉及亲密关系创伤，不面向未成年人，也未有意收集未成年人的任何信息。如发现未成年人向我们提供了个人信息，可通过下方邮箱联系我们删除。

9. 你的权利

你可以随时访问、导出（分享 / 导出 PDF）或删除你的全部本地数据；随时撤回对第三方 AI 共享的同意（设置内开关，或卸载应用）。因我们无法识别你的身份，其他法定权利以可执行的方式通过删除数据实现。

10. 政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策，更新后将在本页面与应用内公布。若变更涉及新的数据共享，我们会在启用前再次征得你的同意。

11. 联系我们

关于隐私的任何疑问，请通过邮箱 mingzhaoluw@gmail.com 联系我们，我们通常在 3 个工作日内回复。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Applies to the iOS and Android app "Narcissist Check (NPD避雷针)". Any updates will be posted on this page.

In short

Your diary, evidence, and practice records stay on your phone only. Only when you actively use an AI feature (Clarity Mirror or Practice Field) and you explicitly agree is your input sent to DeepSeek, a third-party AI provider, to generate the analysis. Originals are discarded immediately after analysis — never stored, never used for ads or profiling. You can withdraw consent anytime in Settings.

1. Overview

This policy explains how Narcissist Check collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. Our core principle is "local-first": your records stay on your own device by default and are not uploaded to our servers. The app requires no account.

2. Information We Collect

  • Content you enter: anchor diary entries (text, images, voice), conversation text and chat screenshots you paste or upload in Clarity Mirror, reasons you write, practice records, and app preferences. These stay on your device by default.
  • Subscription status: membership status from the App Store / Google Play, used to unlock features. We never handle your payment card details.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, or any directly identifying information.

3. AI Features & Third-Party Data Sharing (Key Section)

What is sent

Only when you use an AI feature: the conversation text you paste or type, the chat screenshots you upload (Clarity Mirror), and the scene you choose and each reply you enter (Practice Field).

Who receives it

DeepSeek (deepseek.com), a third-party AI provider. Apart from DeepSeek and the app-store payment platforms, we do not share your content with any other third party.

How it is used

Only to produce that analysis or practice feedback. Originals are discarded immediately after analysis; neither we nor DeepSeek use them for advertising, profiling, or model training.

Consent

  • On first launch, the app shows a dedicated screen explaining what is sent, who receives it, and how it is used. You must explicitly choose "Agree & Share" or "Not now (local only)" before entering the app.
  • Choosing "Not now" does not affect local features (diary, evidence, library). When you later try an AI feature, the app shows the notice again and asks for your consent.
  • You can view the consent status, enable it, or withdraw it anytime in "Settings - AI Data Sharing".

Third-party protection

DeepSeek, as the data recipient, protects your data to a standard at least equivalent to this policy (see DeepSeek's official privacy policy). Except as necessary to generate the results, neither we nor DeepSeek use your conversation content for advertising, profiling, or model training. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unrelated to the analysis.

4. Storage & Security

  • Local data is stored encrypted on your device (iOS Keychain / Android encrypted storage, with an additional app-layer encryption).
  • A privacy lock (PIN and Face / Touch ID) protects app entry; forgetting the PIN erases all local data (by design — it cannot be recovered).
  • The current version offers no cloud sync; your data is never uploaded to any developer server. If cloud sync is introduced in the future, we will explain it and obtain your separate consent before enabling it.

5. System Permissions

  • Photo library: to pick chat screenshots for pattern analysis. Images are used only for that analysis and deleted immediately afterwards.
  • Microphone: to record voice notes in the anchor diary. Recordings stay on your phone only.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: to unlock the privacy lock. Biometric data is processed by the system; the app cannot access it.

You can revoke any permission in system settings at any time. Revoking only disables the corresponding feature; other features and local data are unaffected.

6. Subscriptions & Payments

Subscriptions are completed via Apple App Store or Google Play in-app purchase; the platform processes payment and we only receive subscription status to unlock features. Renewals, refunds, and restoration follow each store's policies.

7. Retention & Deletion

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling permanently deletes all local data and cannot be undone — export anything you want to keep first.
  • "Settings - Clear Data" deletes all diaries, evidence, and settings in one tap and cannot be undone.
  • Content sent to DeepSeek is not retained: it is discarded after analysis and we keep no copy.

8. Minors

The app deals with intimate-relationship trauma and is not directed at minors, nor do we knowingly collect any information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us personal information, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

You may access, export (share / export PDF), or delete all of your local data at any time, and withdraw your consent to third-party AI sharing at any time (Settings toggle, or uninstalling the app). Because we cannot identify you, other statutory rights are exercised in practice through data deletion.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will publish updates on this page and in the app. If a change involves new data sharing, we will ask for your consent again before enabling it.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: mingzhaoluw@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 business days.
