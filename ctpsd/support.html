常见问题

为什么首次使用 AI 功能前会询问我是否同意共享数据？

「澄清镜」与「练习场」的分析由第三方 AI 服务商 DeepSeek（deepseek.com）完成。使用这些功能时，你输入的对话文本、截图或练习回应会发送给 DeepSeek 用于生成分析；原文分析后立即删除，不留存、不用于广告或画像。这是法规与应用商店的要求：发送前必须告诉你发什么、发给谁，并取得你的同意。你可以在设置中随时开启或撤回同意（设置 - AI 数据共享）。

我不同意共享数据，还能用什么功能？

可以。拒绝共享后，日记、证据拱廊、图书馆、灯塔等全部本地功能不受影响；仅 AI 分析与练习场不可用。之后你想用 AI 功能时，应用会再次说明并请求授权，随时可以改变主意。

我的记录会上传到服务器吗？

不会。当前版本不提供云同步，你的日记、截图、录音与设置全部只保存在你的手机上（加密存储）。卸载应用会永久删除全部本地数据且无法恢复——如需保留，请先在应用内导出。

忘记隐私锁的 PIN 怎么办？

出于隐私设计，PIN 无法找回。你可以在锁屏界面选择「忘记 PIN」，此操作会清空本机全部数据并重置应用。这是为了确保拿到你手机的人也无法绕过隐私锁。

分析结果准确吗？

分析仅供自我觉察参考，不构成任何医疗或心理诊断，也并非所有摩擦都是操控——健康的争吵同样存在。请结合自身处境判断，必要时寻求专业帮助。

FAQ

Why does the app ask for my consent before using AI features?

The analysis in Clarity Mirror and the Practice Field is performed by DeepSeek (deepseek.com), a third-party AI provider. When you use these features, the conversation text, screenshots, or practice replies you enter are sent to DeepSeek to generate the analysis. Originals are discarded immediately afterwards — never stored, never used for ads or profiling. Regulations and app store rules require us to tell you what is sent, who receives it, and to obtain your permission before sending. You can enable or withdraw consent anytime in Settings - AI Data Sharing.

Can I still use the app if I decline data sharing?

Yes. Declining does not affect local features — diary, evidence gallery, library, and lighthouse all work. Only AI analysis and the Practice Field are unavailable. When you try an AI feature later, the app will explain again and ask for your consent; you can always change your mind.

Are my records uploaded to a server?

No. The current version offers no cloud sync. Your diary, screenshots, recordings, and settings stay on your phone only (encrypted). Uninstalling permanently deletes all local data and cannot be undone — export anything you want to keep first.

I forgot my privacy-lock PIN

By design, the PIN cannot be recovered. Choose "Forgot PIN" on the lock screen; this erases all local data and resets the app. This guarantees no one holding your phone can bypass the lock.

How accurate are the analyses?

Analyses are for self-awareness only — not a medical or psychological diagnosis. Not every friction is manipulation; healthy conflict exists too. Trust your own judgment and seek professional help when needed.
