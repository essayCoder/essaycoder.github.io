快速上手

  1. 创建出生档案：点击档案图标，填写昵称（可选）、出生日期、出生时间、出生地；或点开地图，在出生地落点自动填入。
  2. 查看解读：八字（四柱）、塔罗、六爻、合盘等多种星盘与占卜。
  3. 每日运势与高级功能：订阅会员后解锁。

Q

完全保存在您的设备上，我们不运营任何收集这些数据的服务器。

中文、英文、日文、韩文等八种语言。

Support

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Need help? Here is a quick start guide and answers to common questions.

Quick start

  1. Create a birth profile: tap the profile icon, enter an optional name, birth date, birth time, and birthplace — or open the map and drop a pin on your birthplace to fill it in automatically.
  2. View readings: BaZi (Four Pillars), tarot, Liu Yao, compatibility charts, and more.
  3. Daily fortune and premium features unlock with a membership.

Frequently asked

Q
Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. We run no servers that collect it.
Q
Why does Kismara ask for location?
To calculate your chart with true solar time and to let you pick a birthplace on the map. You can enter the place manually instead — no permission needed.
Q
I bought VIP but it is not active?
Open the membership screen and tap "Restore Purchases". If it still fails, contact us with your App Store receipt.
Q
Will I lose data if I change phones?
Yes — data is local only, with no cloud sync. Re-enter your profiles on the new device, or export before switching.
Q
Which languages are supported?
Eight languages including Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean.

빠른 시작

  1. 출생 프로필 만들기: 프로필 아이콘을 누르고 별명(선택), 생년월일, 출생 시각, 출생지를 입력합니다. 지도를 열어 출생지에 핀을 놓으면 자동으로 입력됩니다.
  2. 해석 보기: 사주(사주팔자), 타로, 육효, 궁합 등.
  3. 매일의 운세와 프리미엄 기능은 멤버십 가입으로 해제됩니다.
